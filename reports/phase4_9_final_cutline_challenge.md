# PRESSURE-X Phase 4.9 — Final Cut-Line Challenge & Selection Readiness

## Purpose
Phase 4.9 compares the current pooled Top-10 cut-off against every candidate ranked 11–20. It combines the pooled balanced rating gap, alternative-weighting results, reliability tier and match-level pressure-versus-normal diagnostics. The goal is to identify whether the current cut-off is defensible and where a selection decision is especially close.

This is a selection-readiness review, not a final submission. It does not change the Phase 4 rating model or create final Unstop Top-10 lists.

## Decision rules used
- The pooled balanced rating remains the primary ranking basis.
- For each role, the rank-10 candidate is the cut-line incumbent. Every rank 11–20 candidate is compared directly with that candidate.
- A positive balanced rating gap means the rank-10 incumbent's rating is higher than the omitted candidate's rating.
- Alternative-weighting gaps are diagnostics only. They are not averaged into the balanced score.
- Match-level favourable-component counts are descriptive. They do not represent statistical significance or causal effects.
- A tiny score gap is treated as a close decision, not as proof of equivalence or a meaningful performance difference.

## Important limitations
1. Rating gaps are differences in the project's composite rating units. They are not p-values, confidence intervals or probabilities of being better.
2. Alternative weighting rankings are sensitivity checks, not independent replications.
3. Match-level medians give each match equal weight and can be unstable when only a few balls are observed in a match.
4. The pressure classifier and Phase 3 metric definitions are inherited; Phase 4.9 does not validate the underlying definition of pressure or the completeness of the source data.
5. No opponent strength, role/position, innings phase, venue or match-context adjustment is added.
6. A player may be a strong candidate despite a weaker metric in one component; final selection should disclose trade-offs rather than cherry-pick favourable indicators.
