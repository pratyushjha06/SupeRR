## 7. Recommended decision protocol

1. Keep the balanced ranking as the primary reproducible ordering.
2. Present the stronger-only shortlist alongside it, not instead of it.
3. For any proposed final candidate, inspect the pressure and normal raw metrics and match counts.
4. Check whether the candidate remains in contention under the relevant alternate weighting schemes.
5. Treat candidates with low pressure exposure, few pressure matches, or a thin normal baseline as conditional picks.
6. Do not claim statistical significance or causal pressure performance from this observational comparison.
