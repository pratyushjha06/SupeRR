# PRESSURE-X Phase 4.7 — Candidate-by-Candidate Review & Shortlist Readiness

## Purpose
Phase 4.7 turns the Phase 4.4–4.6 diagnostics into an auditable review of all 20 pooled batter candidates and all 20 pooled bowler candidates. It identifies who merits priority review, who needs qualification, and who should remain in reserve. It does **not** select or publish the final Top 10.

## Review rubric (transparent, not an official competition rubric)
The following are screening labels to organize human review, not new rating scores or automatic acceptance/rejection rules.

- **Priority for final review:** pooled rank 1–10, stronger reliability tier, standard uncertainty flag, and top-10 under at least two of the three alternative weighting schemes.
- **Conditional final review:** pooled rank 1–10 but one or more of the above conditions is not met. Candidate remains under consideration with a specific caveat.
- **Reserve candidate:** pooled rank 11–20, stronger reliability tier, and top-10 under at least two alternative weighting schemes.
- **Lower-priority reserve:** pooled rank 11–20 and does not meet the reserve screen above.

Format coverage is separately described as both formats having at least 30 pressure balls/legal balls, or only one format having that exposure. A format-concentration flag is raised if one format contributes at least 80% of pressure exposure. Year-level recurrence is reported only where Phase 4.5 produced a pooled Top-10 recurrence row; absence of a row for ranks 11–20 means **not included in that Phase 4.5 recurrence tracking set**, not zero recurrence.

## Important interpretation rules
1. The labels are review workflow categories, not a replacement for the underlying ratings.
2. An alternate-weighting top-10 count measures rank stability under the three tested weighting schemes; it does not prove statistical significance.
3. Favourable component counts are directional diagnostics only. They do not account for metric covariance, role, batting position, match context or uncertainty and must not be treated as another composite score.
4. Format-specific robust ratings must not be averaged. The pooled rating remains the common ranking basis; format-specific data is supporting evidence.
5. Format concentration is not a disqualifier. It identifies where a candidate's evidence is mostly coming from.
6. A limited candidate can still be selected if the final evidence case is compelling, but the report should explicitly disclose the limitation.
